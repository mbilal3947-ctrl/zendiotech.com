Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. Introduction

Zendiotech respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you visit our website or communicate with us through our services.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with Zendiotech, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or mobile number.
  • Company or organisation details.
  • Information contained in enquiries or messages.
  • Information necessary to respond to service requests.

We may also receive limited technical information associated with your use of our website, such as browser or device information, where technically necessary or where applicable.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests.

4. Data Sharing

Zendiotech does not intend to sell personal information for monetary consideration. Information may be shared with service providers or other parties where reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, protect our systems, comply with applicable law, or otherwise fulfil a legitimate business purpose.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, for legitimate business requirements, or as required by applicable law.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the information involved, individuals may have rights relating to their personal data, including rights concerning access, correction, withdrawal of consent, or grievance redressal.

Requests relating to personal information may be submitted using the contact details provided on our website.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party technologies or services for hosting, security, analytics, communications or other operational purposes. Such third parties may process information in accordance with their own applicable policies and contractual obligations.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements or practices. Updated versions will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy-related request, please contact Zendiotech through the contact information provided on our Contact page.
